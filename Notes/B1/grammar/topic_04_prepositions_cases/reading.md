  ## Place: Location and Direction
  *Alex asks where a book is, then where Sam is going with it.*

  **Alex**: Where is the report? Is it on the desk?

  **Sam**: No. It is in the bag by the door.

  **Alex**: Are you going to the meeting with it?

  **Sam**: Yes. I am going into the room on the second floor.

  **Alex**: Leave a copy on my desk before you go.

  **Sam**: I will put it on the desk, and I will take the original into the meeting.

  **Alex**: Is the bag by the door or against the wall?

  **Sam**: By the door, on the floor. It is not on the wall. I am walking toward the stairs now.

  **Alex**: When you go into the room, put the copy on my desk, not in the drawer.

  **Sam**: On the desk. I will take the original with me into the meeting, and I will leave the copy behind me.

  **Alex**: After the meeting, come back to this floor. Do not leave the original in the room upstairs.

  **Sam**: I will bring it back and put it in the bag by the door, where it was.

  **Alex**: From the bag, to my desk, into the meeting, and back to the bag. Is that the path?

  **Sam**: Yes. I am at the stairs now. The copy will be on your desk before I go into the room.
