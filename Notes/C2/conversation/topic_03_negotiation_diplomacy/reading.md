  **Alex**: Your side wants the border road open by June. Ours wants it checked before any opening.

  **Sam**: If I call your wish a delay, your side loses face. How do we say it?

  **Sam**: We can accept a check on the first of May, and an opening on the first of June if the check is clear.

  **Alex**: Write both dates, and write that neither side admits fault. The frame is the road, not the blame.

  **Sam**: We will sign that wording and nothing wider.

  **Alex**: Write that too. A check that is not clear holds the road closed. It names no guilty side.

  **Alex**: Right. Neither side admits fault. The frame stays the road.

  **Sam**: Our side wants one more line. The check looks only at the road surface.
