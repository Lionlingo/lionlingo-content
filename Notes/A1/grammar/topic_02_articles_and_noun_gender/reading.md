  *In a bookshop, Alex asks for one particular book.*

  **Alex**: Excuse me, I am looking for a book about the city.

  **Sam**: Any book, or the one on the front table?

  **Alex**: The one on the front table. A friend told me about it.

  **Sam**: That book is already sold. I can show you a new copy.

  **Alex**: Yes, please. Is the new one the same story?

  **Sam**: Yes. It is a new copy of the same book.

  **Alex**: How much is the new copy?

  **Sam**: Twelve euros. I can hold it at this counter while you look.

  **Alex**: I will take it. Is there a bag for one book?

  **Sam**: Yes. One small bag is free. The large bag costs one euro.

  **Alex**: The small bag, please. Is the book on the front table the only story about the city?

  **Sam**: No. There is also a thin book of photographs. It is a different book, not another copy.

  **Alex**: Not today. I only need this copy of the story my friend named.

  **Sam**: Here it is, in the small bag. The receipt is inside.

  ## Language tips

  - a before consonant sound: a book, a university
  - an before vowel sound: an apple, an hour
  - the = specific or already mentioned
  - No gender on English nouns - the never changes
  - a/an = one general thing; the = this specific one

  - das = spezifisch oder bereits erwähnt
  - Kein Geschlecht bei englischen Substantiven - das ändert sich nie
  - a/an = eine allgemeine Sache; the = dieses bestimmte
