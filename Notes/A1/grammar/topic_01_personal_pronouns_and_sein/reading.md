  ## Personal Pronouns and To Be
  *Alex and Sam look at a photo and say who each person is.*

  **Sam**: Who is she?

  **Alex**: She is my sister. She is a teacher.

  **Sam**: And him, next to her?

  **Alex**: He is my brother. He is tall, and he is a student.

  **Sam**: Are they both at the same school?

  **Alex**: No. She works there. He studies there. We are a small family.

  **Sam**: Are you a teacher too?

  **Alex**: No. I am a cook. She is the teacher. He is the student. We are not the same.

  **Sam**: Is this your photo, or is it theirs?

  **Alex**: It is ours. My mother took it. She is not in the photo.

  **Sam**: Where is she now?

  **Alex**: She is at home. He is at school. I am here with you.

  **Sam**: They look happy. You look like her.

  **Alex**: People say that. She is short, and I am short. He is the tall one.

  **Alex**: 彼女は家にいます。彼は学校です。私はここにあなたといます。

  **Sam**: 彼らは嬉しそうです。あなたは彼女に似ています。

  **Alex**: よく言われます。彼女は背が低く、私も低いです。彼が背の高いほうです。

en:
  ## Language tips

  - I = first person singular
  - you = second person (one form for formal and informal)
  - he / she / it = third person singular
  - To be: I am, you are, he/she/it is, we are, they are
  - Every sentence needs a subject: I am here (not *Am here)
de:
  ## Sprachtipps

  - I = erste Person Singular
  - du = zweite Person (eine Form für formell und informell)
  - er/sie/es = dritte Person Singular
  - Sein: Ich bin, du bist, er/sie/es ist, wir sind, sie sind
  - Jeder Satz braucht ein Subjekt: Ich bin hier (nicht *Bin hier)

  ## Astuces de langue

  - I = première personne du singulier
  - vous = deuxième personne (un formulaire pour formel et informel)
  - il/elle/il = troisième personne du singulier
